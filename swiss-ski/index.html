A / DRoll onto your edges and turn — hold longer for a tighter carve
WTuck at speed · skate and pole when slow
SPizza / snowplough: tips together, gentle braking at any speed, steer with A/D
QHockey stop: skis thrown across, hard stop · hold A or D with Q to choose the side
SpaceJump · on a rail or box: pop off
In the airA/D spin · W/S front/back flip (press after take-off) · Shift grab · land a box or rail to slide it
E / FRide the lift at a valley station · fast-forward the ride
GTouring skis: skins on / off — walk uphill with W (Shift faster)
B / PAvalanche transceiver drill: find the buried beacon, then probe and dig (P)
OWardrobe: helmets, jackets, trousers, boots, goggles and ski designs, with a live preview
KTake your skis off and walk (W, Shift to jog) · put them back on
RAfter a minor fall: get back up with your skis on. After a bad crash the ski patrol comes for you
F3Show the frame rate (Settings → Quality preset for Low / Medium / High / Ultra)
T / MTeleport list · zoomable map (scroll, drag, click to drop in)
CCamera: follow · first person (goggles) · high · helmet cam · GoPro on a pole (fisheye)
VReplay of the last 30 s from cinematic cameras: Space pause, ← → skip, S slow motion, C camera, Enter save the clip, V or Esc back
HPhoto mode: drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, W A S D Q E to move; lens, depth of field, filters, frames and captions; Enter takes the photo (it goes to the album in the main menu); also a time-lapse of the whole day
Ski toursT → Ski tours: touring skis and skins on at the start of a marked (pink) route to a real summit with a cross and a summit book
X (hold)Slow motion (big jumps and bad crashes slow down by themselves — Settings → Interface)
BackspaceRestart the run from where it started (lift exit, teleport, race start)
